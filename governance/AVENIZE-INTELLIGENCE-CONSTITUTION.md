# AVENIZE INTELLIGENCE CONSTITUTION

## Principle
AI exists to improve business outcomes while remaining subordinate to product, security, data and autonomy governance.

## AI must
- use canonical business context;
- respect tenant and role boundaries;
- distinguish facts from inference;
- preserve provenance for consequential outputs;
- express uncertainty when evidence is incomplete;
- avoid fabricating database state, customer activity or financial facts;
- make consequential actions reviewable.

## Action boundary
AI may recommend broadly but may execute only actions allowed by the autonomy policy for that action class.

## Memory
Memory must have scope, ownership, retention and deletion semantics. Cross-tenant leakage is a critical security incident.

## Evaluation
Critical AI workflows require deterministic checks around schema validity, authorization, tool permissions and postconditions. AI output alone is never proof that an operation succeeded.
